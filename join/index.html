Graduate application materials are reviewed each semester.

GRADUATE APPLICATIONS

How to apply

Email the following documents to Professor Hongjo Kim. Applicants will receive a reply by email after their materials have been reviewed.

  1. A personal statement explaining your motivation for applying
  2. An academic transcript including your class rank
  3. A curriculum vitae (CV)
Contact Professor Kim ↗

hongjo@yonsei.ac.kr

International applicants

Master’s applicants

Applicants are encouraged to apply for the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) first and to apply to the lab after securing GKS funding.

Ph.D. applicants

Ph.D. applicants are also encouraged to apply to the lab after being selected for GKS.

Ph.D. applicants without GKS funding

As of September 2026, a Yonsei University support program for international students is available for the next two years. Ph.D. applicants without GKS funding may therefore be considered based on their qualifications.

Recommendation letters for GKS applications

If a recommendation letter is needed during the application process, please email your CV, academic transcript, and personal statement.
